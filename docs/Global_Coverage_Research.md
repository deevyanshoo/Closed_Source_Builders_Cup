# Global sourcing coverage: evidence and implementation boundary

Research checked 6 October 2026 for the 18 October prototype deadline. The architecture should impose **no arbitrary cap on countries or currencies**. It should expose the full provider catalog, then compute an answer only where a particular product–origin–destination–period has the necessary observations. “In catalog,” “historical data retrieved,” “model forecast produced,” and “transaction quote verified” are four separate states.

| Layer | Credible breadth now | Limitation that must appear in product behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Countries and products | UN Comtrade says it holds reported goods/services trade data from **nearly 200 countries and areas** since 1962; reporter, partner, product, flow, and period can be queried. [UN Comtrade overview](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/welcome-to-un-comtrade/) | This is a catalog-level claim. Availability varies by reporter, HS code, annual/monthly period, and release date. There is no guaranteed current observation for every route. [Data availability](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/data-availability/) |
| FX | Frankfurter v2 advertises **223 currencies** from **104 central banks and official sources**, with daily updates and histories whose earliest series reaches 1948. Its `/v2/currencies` endpoint supplies provider coverage. [Frankfurter v2](https://frankfurter.dev/) | 223 is catalog breadth, not 223 simultaneously fresh, directly quoted or ECB-backed pairs. Default results blend sources; some source series are weekly/monthly/quarterly. Rates are informational mid rates, not executable FX quotes. [Frankfurter methods and FAQ](https://frankfurter.dev/) |
| ECB FX | ECB publishes daily EUR reference rates for **30 currencies**, generally around 16:00 CET on working days; its site offers CSV/XML time series. [ECB reference rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html) | ECB rates are informational, not transaction rates. RUB reference publication remains suspended. An ECB-only mode must expose just its observed coverage; Frankfurter's wider catalog draws from other providers. |
| Commodity benchmark | World Bank Pink Sheet publishes a [current monthly workbook](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/74e8be41ceb20fa0da750cda2f6b9e4e-0050012026/related/CMO-Historical-Data-Monthly.xlsx) and [October 2026 PDF](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/74e8be41ceb20fa0da750cda2f6b9e4e-0050012026/related/CMO-Pink-Sheet-October-2026.pdf), with September 2026 monthly values. [World Bank data page](https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets) | These are specific benchmark grade/location prices, mostly USD per named unit, not universal product prices or supplier offers. A sampled source page shows **7 base-metal/iron-ore lines** (aluminum, copper, iron ore, lead, nickel, tin, zinc) and **3 precious-metal lines** (gold, platinum, silver). It has no lithium, cobalt, manganese, graphite, rare earth, or steel price line; do not infer one from a metal index. Barley, sorghum, and shrimp are listed but show no recent values in the October PDF. Source descriptions and revision notes matter. |
| Tariffs | WTO's Tariff & Trade Data platform describes official schedules for **over 150 economies**, including bound, MFN applied, and preferential rates, generally keyed by HS product and year. [WTO tariff data](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tariffs_e/tariff_data_e.htm) | “Bound” is a ceiling, not the payable duty. A preferential rate is conditional on origin/rules; HS-6 is insufficient for many national tariff lines. No live landed-duty claim until destination-specific current tariff line, origin eligibility, taxes and trade measures are checked. The [WTO API portal](https://apiportal.wto.org/) offers a free key for time-series market-access indicators, but that does not by itself establish a current transaction rate. |
| Freight | Freightos Baltic Index tracks **12 regional ocean-container lanes** and 40-foot equivalent container benchmarks. [FBX coverage](https://www.freightos.com/enterprise/terminal/freightos-baltic-index-global-container-pricing-index/) | An index/region is not a door-to-door quote for a specific origin, destination, mode, cargo, date, or Incoterm. The provider's [data terms](https://www.freightos.com/freightos-data-terms-and-conditions/) govern reuse; do not scrape or present it as a comprehensive public quote API. |

## Trade data and price interpretation

UN Comtrade's free registered tier currently advertises **500 API calls/day**, **1 call/second**, and up to **100,000 records per data-API call**; its keyless preview is limited to **500 records** and a narrow query. [Subscription plans](https://comtradeplus.un.org/Subscriptions), [API documentation](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/un-comtrade-api/). Build cached, selected queries and inspect [data availability](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/data-availability/) before requests. An account/key is a separate setup step; the research did not obtain one or verify a production key.

Trade value divided by net weight/quantity is a **historical customs unit value**, not a current commodity market price or supplier quote. UN Comtrade says quantity/weight may be standardized or estimated, outliers removed, and unreliable estimates suppressed. Keep unit, estimation flags, flow and HS revision with the result; suppress invalid or mixed-unit ratios. [Quantity and weight methodology](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/quantity-and-weight-data-in-un-comtrade/). Imports are commonly CIF-type and exports FOB-type, but national practice differs; `primaryValue` can select a different available field. Comparing them or adding freight can double count services. [UN Comtrade valuation](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/trade-valuation/). Releases and revisions lack a fixed country schedule. [Data availability](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/data-availability/).

## Recommended delivery sequence before 18 October

1. **Full searchable catalog, strict eligibility.** Load all current Frankfurter currency metadata, UN reporter/partner references and HS product taxonomy without a UI cap. Record which pair/route/product-period observations actually exist. Search should still return catalog entries when data is missing, with a clear reason and a next action.
2. **Evidence-backed historical comparison.** Ingest the Pink Sheet monthly workbook with per-series description, unit and last non-null month; use Frankfurter v2 with provider, timestamp and base/quote attribution. Expose World Bank benchmark price only for exact commodity/grade matches. Show Comtrade origin rankings from verified reporter-product-flow-period data and label customs unit values as historical proxies.
3. **Confidence and freshness gates.** For each recommendation, display source, observation period, data age, units, and which inputs are observed versus assumed. Do not generate a numeric total when FX, commodity unit, tariff, freight or quantity basis is incompatible or absent. Let a user enter a supplier price/freight/tariff assumption with a visible “user supplied” badge and sensitivity range.
4. **Tariff/freight enrichment after core data works.** Add WTO tariff lookup for specific destination + national HS line + origin + effective date only where verified; otherwise show “tariff unavailable—check official customs schedule.” Add licensed lane index or a freight quote integration only where route/mode match; otherwise show “freight quote required.” Neither should be filled with a generic global constant.
5. **Forecast scope follows backtests.** If a forecast is implemented, first define eligible series with enough continuous history, evaluate rolling out-of-sample errors, and show interval and horizon. A displayed catalog entry does not imply forecastability. Until that evidence exists, present historical trend and scenario sensitivity, not a global forecast coverage claim.

This is a source capability review, not a completed integration test: the World Bank workbook link was identified from the official page but its binary contents were not parsed here; no Comtrade API key, WTO tariff-line lookup, freight quote or transaction-specific FX was exercised.
