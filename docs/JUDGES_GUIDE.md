# Welcome to ZOORQ — a product by ClosedSource
*See disruption. Close the stock gap.*

## Try it in 2 minutes — no signup
1. Open **Solara Cables** to start with a populated procurement workspace, or explore any of the 10 companies.
2. Open **News** for company-relevant stories, sources, dates and business implications. Switch to **Global Watch** for wider trade, route and geopolitical signals. Public news refreshes hourly.
3. Select **Talk to ZOORQ**. Try your mother tongue—the Gemini Live operator is multilingual and uses your current company’s workspace. Try English, Hindi, Punjabi, Haryanvi, Portuguese or Spanish. Use **Type to ZOORQ** if your microphone is unavailable.

## Try asking
- “What’s happening around the world that can affect my procurement?”
- “Which inventory risks need my attention, and why?”
- “Does our current plan prevent a stockout before the selected delivery?”
- “मेरी कंपनी की खरीद पर कौन-सी खबरें असर डाल सकती हैं?”
- “Explain that in Hindi.”

## Explore ZOORQ, feature by feature
- **Live News:** Public sources are collected hourly and mapped to your company’s materials, industry and routes, with source links and dates.
- **Global Watch:** Connect geopolitical events, trade disruptions and chokepoints to potential business exposure.
- **Multilingual voice & chat:** Ask, analyse and prepare actions naturally—try your mother tongue.
- **Quote intelligence:** Gemini reads supplier documents and extracts terms with source evidence for your review.
- **Inventory intelligence:** See stock cover, delivery timing and the material gap that could interrupt production.
- **Sourcing comparisons:** Compare reviewed supplier quotes, costs and arrival dates against your purchasing needs.
- **Delay recovery & what-if:** Test a supplier delay and inspect its cost and supply impact before applying a change.
- **Maps & route simulation:** Explore supported sea and land supply corridors on Google Maps.
- **Cost–carbon comparison:** Weigh sourcing cost alongside supported emissions estimates and their assumptions.
- **Commodity & FX predictions:** Explore dated forecast ranges and uncertainty as context for smarter raw-material purchasing and FX timing.
- **RFQ & email drafts:** Prepare reviewable, saved, unsent drafts; external internet research asks for your consent.
- **10 company workspaces:** Explore 52 material scenarios across different manufacturing sectors.

**Powered by Gemini on Vertex AI, Gemini Live, Cloud Run, Firestore and Google Maps.**
