# Welcome to ZOORQ — a product by ClosedSource
*See disruption. Close the stock gap.*

## Try it in 2 minutes — no signup
1. Open **Solara Cables** to start with a populated procurement workspace, or explore any of the 10 companies.
2. Open **News** for company-relevant stories, sources, dates and business implications. Switch to **Global Watch** for wider trade, route and geopolitical signals. Public news refreshes hourly.
3. Select **Talk to ZOORQ**. Speak naturally in your preferred language—the Gemini Live operator is multilingual and uses your current company’s workspace. Use **Type to ZOORQ** if your microphone is unavailable.

## Try asking
- “What’s happening around the world that can affect my procurement?”
- “Which inventory risks need my attention, and why?”
- “Does our current plan prevent a stockout before the selected delivery?”
- “मेरी कंपनी की खरीद पर कौन-सी खबरें असर डाल सकती हैं?”
- “Explain that in Hindi.”

## Explore further
Inventory & supply shows stock cover and routes; Procurement compares quotes; Drafts holds saved RFQs and emails. Drafts are unsent. Review proposed actions before applying them. External internet research asks for your consent.

**Powered by Gemini on Vertex AI, Gemini Live, Cloud Run, Firestore and Google Maps.**
