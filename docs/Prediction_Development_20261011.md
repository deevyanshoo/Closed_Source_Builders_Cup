# Monthly price projection development — 11 October 2026

The reusable `procure.forecast.monthly_projection` module accepts a caller-owned, consecutive monthly price panel and explicit per-series unit, family, source, retrieval-time and error-reference metadata. It reads no files or services. Each entry captures UTC time once, rejects retrieval after that time or with a nonzero UTC offset, and requires the final observed month to precede the captured UTC month. `assemble_persistence` always selects the last observed native price for horizons 1, 3 and 6: an issue in the month after the final observation, with targets two, four and seven months after that observation. Its generation timestamp and UTC as-of date use the same captured time. If the issue month is not the current UTC month, the result says `HISTORICAL_NOT_CURRENT`; it never relabels an older panel as today's forecast.

The optional `ridge_candidates` method fits three fixed direct cumulative-log-return models per series (alpha 10; six trailing monthly-return features; training anchors with all seven subsequent labels observed). The optional `foundation_candidates` method accepts already injected, plain-list prediction callables and checks every quantile and lead before exposing median point candidates for Chronos-Bolt-Tiny, Chronos2 independent, and Chronos2 family-joint. These methods do not load weights. All candidate rows say `EXPERIMENTAL_NOT_PROMOTED`; calling the baseline method does not invoke either candidate method. Native prices are strings with each series' unit and source provenance, not a cross-currency aggregate.

The observed development comparison of ten fixed candidates retained persistence overall (`outputs/Prediction_All_Models_Comparison_20261011.json`, `outputs/Prediction_All_Models_Selection_Diagnostic_20261011.json` in the control-room workspace). The post-hoc combined calibration selector's exposed development mean absolute log-return gain versus persistence was **−58.51%** in fold A and **+0.79%** in fold B; its fold A World Bank Copper/Aluminum same-unit native-price guard failed. Those numbers describe that historical screen, not a validated improvement. Individual raw models may improve specific assets or horizons in that screen; choosing them after looking at it is exploratory. Native errors across USD per metric ton and distinct FX units cannot be pooled into one meaningful number.

The panel evidence is latest-vintage, development windows are exposed, the USD-linked series are dependent, and horizons overlap. Foundation pretraining overlap is unknown. No untouched future holdout, point-in-time data vintage, calibrated interval, current market price, accuracy guarantee, or procurement savings has been established. A separate reviewed, authorized private runner would be needed to adapt real model tensors to the plain-list callable and to produce any source-backed context. This module is not wired into the app, an API, planning, or a production selector.
