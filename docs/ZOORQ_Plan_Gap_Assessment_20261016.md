## Taher's carbon implementation and its gate

PR #114 contains Taher Afsar's [implementation commit `7a29bae`](https://github.com/deevyanshoo/Closed_Source_Builders_Cup/commit/7a29bae5b21401a3e6f826eb50468b8519953b41) and [package fix `2eb31e7`](https://github.com/deevyanshoo/Closed_Source_Builders_Cup/commit/2eb31e730b8f5df1981aed451048d73231b88db1). The [calculator](https://github.com/deevyanshoo/Closed_Source_Builders_Cup/blob/2eb31e730b8f5df1981aed451048d73231b88db1/procurement-engine/src/procure/carbon/calculator.py) computes material mass in tonnes × kg CO2e/tonne and entered transport tonne-km × mode factor using Decimal. Unknown factors and non-mass units become explicit unavailable/mismatch states; a complete combined figure appears only when both components are estimable. Its API binds an estimate to the active company/plan and rejects a stale plan. The [registry](https://github.com/deevyanshoo/Closed_Source_Builders_Cup/blob/2eb31e730b8f5df1981aed451048d73231b88db1/procurement-engine/config/carbon_factors_v1.json) cites the UK DESNZ 2026 full spreadsheet, file hash, licence, factor year, sheet, row, and units for five material mappings and five freight modes. These source claims and values were read from the PR; they have not been independently rechecked against the publisher in this assessment. PR #114 reports 736 Python tests, 372 web tests, and local desktop/mobile browser checks, not hosted acceptance.

Before integrating #114, verify the cited workbook values and licence directly; decide which material mappings are defensible; provide measured mass for counted vials/cartons/foil or preserve `unit_mismatch`; and resolve the stated boundary overlap. The registry notes that its material factors already include transport to point of sale, so adding buyer-entered legs can double count. PET's published category includes forming and may overstate unformed resin. The estimate must keep its UK-average, synthetic-input, entered-distance, and non-certified labels. Review the `app.py` hooks and deploy-package allowlist against #106 and #118, then run combined backend/web checks and an exact-revision hosted cost-and-carbon scenario, including missing-factor and stale-plan states. [Issue #108](https://github.com/deevyanshoo/Closed_Source_Builders_Cup/issues/108) remains open until that delivery evidence exists.

PR #115 is stacked on #114 and also authored by Taher ([commit `4abc75b`](https://github.com/deevyanshoo/Closed_Source_Builders_Cup/commit/4abc75b586b274e1d97be5a512d4d3706e9792c2)). It supplies an official-link scheme registry and indicative formulas, while explicitly declining eligibility decisions and certificate claims. Its possible financial/legal interpretation merits a separate authoritative-source and formula review. It is outside the initial scenario-emissions comparison needed for a narrow hackathon demonstration; [#109](https://github.com/deevyanshoo/Closed_Source_Builders_Cup/issues/109) remains open.
