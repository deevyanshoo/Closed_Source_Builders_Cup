## #116 — country commodity pricing: **defer hosted merge pending product/data fixes; reuse calculation and provenance pattern**

- **P1, “country-wise” overclaims.** `apps/web/src/demo-country-pricing.tsx:30-45` heads the panel “Commodity pricing by country,” but `country_pricing.py:92-111,129-142` produces one global World Bank monthly series per material, then converts it into company currencies; the country field applies only to separate synthetic supplier quotes. FX conversion is not an observed country-specific commodity price. Rename the section “Global benchmarks and synthetic origin quotes” and keep the two distinct. The World Bank [commodity markets page](https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets) confirms an October 2, 2026 monthly update. The exact linked workbook was independently checked against the bundled snapshot; all 18 copied values, headers and units match (cell audit below).
- **P1, stale response can cross company/session scope.** `demo-country-pricing.tsx:21-28` accepts any completed fetch into parent state; `demo-page.tsx:289,660` retains that state across company switching and checks only `company_id`. `country_pricing.py:136-159` omits `session_id`/`context_version` from the response. A slow A request can settle after switching A→B→A, and an old A result then appears with obsolete offers and FX. Follow the existing news/operator epoch and context-version guard; clear pricing on session/company changes and validate both response and request scope.
- **P2, thin ten-company coverage.** The snapshot has six benchmark keys (`copper`, `aluminium`, `wheat`, `sugar`, `cotton`, `oil`), while six of ten fictional companies have **zero** matched public benchmark materials (TerraPack, Meridian, GridSpring, CircuitPeak, Atlas, StoneBridge). Current material IDs are matched exactly (`country_pricing.py:93`). The UI does honestly say “no public benchmark”; acceptance must show all ten company states and avoid claiming universal commodity coverage.
- **P2, incomplete saved-offer representation.** `country_pricing.py:114-126` takes only `offer['items'][0]`. The quote model and current review UI support multiple items (`apps/web/src/demo-page.tsx:188-190`). A saved multi-line quote silently omits later priced materials from “Accepted quotes by origin”; iterate eligible items or explicitly label first-line-only coverage.
- **P2, FX hardening.** `country_pricing.py:31-60` checks dates but does not ensure the EUR/USD and EUR/target observations share a provider; formula always calls them ECB. It also divides by an unvalidated USD rate. Current `demo/news.py:235-236` emits ECB rows, so the normal adapter narrows this risk, but validate finite positive decimal rates and matching provider before the endpoint is used with future sources.
- **Positive:** Decimal conversion, tonne/kg change, date/provenance fields, missing-FX state, separate synthetic quotes, cookie-backed session route, and package manifest are useful. `prepare_package.py:16` includes the JSON asset. The endpoint is a read-only GET guarded by the existing session helper; no new action bypass was observed. Do not present the monthly benchmark as a live price.
