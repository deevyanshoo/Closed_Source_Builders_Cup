# Local arrival-change material screen

`project_arrival_change(snapshot, scenario)` is a pure, synthetic, internal projection. It does not ingest events, apply a plan, persist an approval, infer a delay from news, or calculate production/dispatch feasibility. Its mapping schema is local to this module and is not a shared HTTP contract.

The snapshot supplies `workspace_id`, `plan_version`, `snapshot_id`, an aware `as_of` instant and `synthetic: true`. `nodes` have unique `id` and `kind` (`shipment`, `purchase_order`, `component`, `production_order`, `commitment`); `edges` use `from`/`to` and follow those five kinds in order. `inventory` lines name a component, unit, and `starting_stock`, which is the complete balance at `as_of`. `arrival_lines` name `line_id`, shipment, purchase order, component, quantity, unit, and `arrival_at`. `commitments` name their ID, production order, `required_at`, `due_at`, and explicit component `demands` with quantity/unit. The scenario supplies matching workspace, `expected_plan_version`, `source_snapshot_id`, `event_id`, `scenario_id`, `synthetic: true`, and `changes` naming line IDs and replacement `arrival_at` instants. Changes alter dates only; quantity is never inferred.

Quantities must be plain nonnegative decimal strings. Timestamps require timezone offsets or `Z`, seconds, and at most six fractional digits. All instants are normalized to UTC; arrivals and requirements before `as_of` are rejected to prevent counting stock twice. Duplicate identical arrival lines and updates normalize; conflicting duplicates reject the whole projection. The complete declared node/edge, inventory, arrival, commitment and demand graph is checked before any numbers are returned, including declared orders outside the changed line's reachable closure. Units must match exactly; no conversion is guessed.

A `screened` result names the source and scenario IDs, normalized `as_of`, normalized `arrival_changes`, all graph-reachable `affected_commitments`, and `component_balances`. Each affected commitment includes every explicit component demand, baseline and changed `used`, `shortfall`, `remaining_stock`, and one of `new_shortage`, `increased_shortage`, `unchanged_shortage`, or `reduced_shortage`. `material_at_risk` means changed material shortfall is positive at `required_at`; it does not assert a missed production or customer due date. Arrivals at a requirement instant are credited first, demand ties are processed by commitment ID, and later receipts cannot erase an earlier shortfall. Balances and shortfalls remain per component/unit; no mixed-unit total is returned. `limitations` state the missing operational constraints. An `unsupported` result has a reason/details and no numerical fields.

This screen deliberately lacks BOM derivation, capacity, maintenance, cash, work in process, dispatch, alternative sourcing, durable replay/idempotency, and approved plan application. Those require separately evidenced inputs and integration tests. The local fixture data is synthetic and is not a provider ETA or a recovery recommendation.
